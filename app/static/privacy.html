Privacy Notice

Last updated: September 25, 2026.

The Path Finder is designed to collect the minimum information needed to generate a personalized decision-support report. We do not need your full name, home address, phone number, government identifier, payment card number, medical details, or other unrelated sensitive information.

Assessment data

Your answers may include your age range, work situation, experience, goals, preferences, available time, practical constraints, and written descriptions you choose to provide. Do not enter names, contact details, SSNs, account numbers, payment details, medical information, or other sensitive personal data in free-text fields.

Stored assessment drafts, completed answers, recommendation results, and progress feedback are encrypted by the application before being written to the database. Private consultation content is retained for up to 90 days from the purchase date so you can resume the assessment, use the report, and complete the Day 7, Day 14, and Day 30 check-ins. After that window, detailed drafts, assessment content, reports, and feedback are removed automatically during privacy maintenance.

Originality protection

To prevent exact reuse of a generated consultation or one of its instruction sections, the service may retain non-identifying cryptographic fingerprints of generated text. These fingerprints are one-way hashes, are not linked to a customer identity or purchase record, and cannot be used to reconstruct the consultation. Detailed assessment and report content remains subject to the retention and deletion rules above.

Private access

Paid access is stored in a secure browser cookie marked HttpOnly, Secure when served over HTTPS, and SameSite. A browser session may hold more than one active consultation so a returning customer can switch between purchases without repurchasing. The private credential is not stored in localStorage and report links do not contain it. Customers may optionally reveal a separate backup reference and key for restoring an active consultation on another browser or device; anyone with both values can restore that consultation while its access window is active.

Payments

Payment card details and checkout contact information are handled by Stripe. This application does not store payment card numbers and does not retain the checkout email in its own database. We retain only the minimum transaction references and payment status needed to operate the purchase and support access.

Advertising measurement

If Meta advertising measurement is enabled, it is loaded only after the visitor accepts the optional advertising measurement prompt. Assessment answers, report contents, private access credentials, and progress feedback must not be sent to advertising measurement services.

Security and minimization

The service uses encrypted assessment storage, parameterized database queries, restricted browser security headers, rate limiting, private cookie sessions, and payment processing through Stripe. No internet service can promise zero security risk, so the product deliberately minimizes the amount of identifying information it holds.

Requests

You may request deletion or ask privacy questions by contacting support. Some minimal transaction records may need to remain where required for accounting, fraud prevention, payment disputes, or other legal obligations.
